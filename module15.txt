Question Number.    1.    Ram effect' due to aircraft forward speed will cause the efficiency of the engine to.      
Option A.    remain constant.

Option C.    increase.  
Correct Answer is.    increase.  
Explanation.    Ram effect improves compression ratio which improves thrust without using extra fuel Rolls Royce The Jet engine Page 219 refers.


Question Number.    2.    The efficiency of a gas turbine engine at altitude.      
Option A.    decreases.  
Option B.    remains constant.  
Option C.    increases.  
Correct Answer is.    increases.  
Explanation.    The compressor performs better at lower air temperatures(see Jeppersen Gas Turbines Page 2-33) thus improving thermal efficiency. Also lower air temperatures up to the tropopause assist in maintaining Propulsive Efficiency(whilst accepting that decreasing density decreases mass flow). Also note that operators fly at the tropopause whenever possible even for short flights for  the best SFC.
